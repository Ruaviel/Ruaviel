# Ruaviel — Progress

## 1. Current Status

Ruaviel is in active development. A public documentation repository exists today, describing the project's covenant, protocol specifications, and deployment history. The immersive sanctuary experience — the cinematic, human-centered environment at the heart of the project — is being developed and tested locally, and is not yet available as a public visitor experience. The humanitarian coordination layer (candle lighting, contributions, relief requests, and the public witness/transparency system) is documented as intended architecture; none of it is currently operational.

## 2. What Exists Today

- **Public documentation**: a covenant statement, a Dual Relief System protocol specification, and a deployment registry, all published and linkable.
- **A locally tested immersive prototype**: a cinematic sanctuary experience, built and iterated on in a local development environment. It has not been deployed for public access.
- **A deployed covenant anchor contract on Base Mainnet**: an on-chain deployment that can be independently verified via the published deployment registry. It is not currently integrated into any live application, and it does not process payouts, treasury activity, hardship decisions, or recipient data.
- **A historical prototype on Base Sepolia using Chainlink Functions**: an earlier, no-longer-active exploration of oracle-based verification, kept as documented history rather than as running infrastructure.
- Documented architecture for the six-region sanctuary, the Dual Relief System, and DAO-style coordination mechanisms. Portions of the sanctuary have locally tested prototypes, while the complete six-region experience and humanitarian coordination systems are not yet implemented.

## 5. Planned Work

- Public deployment of the immersive sanctuary experience, on a timeline not yet set.
- Technical implementation of the Dual Relief System: contribution handling, relief request intake, and the human-stewardship workflows that would sit behind them.
- Design and implementation of the treasury mechanisms described in the protocol specification, ahead of any public fundraising.
- Integration of blockchain verification (Base, and potentially Chainlink) into the live application, once the humanitarian layer it would support actually exists.
- Establishing and documenting the legal and organizational structure Ruaviel would operate under before any public fundraising or relief operations. That structure has not been finalized or publicly announced.

## 6. What Is Not Yet Operational

To be explicit about the boundary between design and operation, matching the project's own published documentation:

- The immersive sanctuary is not open to public visitors.
- Candle lighting and voluntary contributions are described mechanisms, not currently available to the public.
- No public hardship-intake channel is currently operational. The Financial Relief and Time Relief pathways remain intended architecture, not services currently available to the public.
- The Mercy Treasury and Stability/Operations Treasury remain documented designs. No public Ruaviel treasury system is currently operational through the application.
- The public witness/transparency layer (aggregate candles-lit, contributions, outcomes reporting) is planned and does not currently publish anything, live or otherwise.
- DAO governance enforcement is not deployed.
- No nonprofit, charitable, tax-exempt, or other special legal status is claimed by this repository or should be inferred from Ruaviel's public materials.

## 7. Development Principles

- **Universal welcome.** Ruaviel is openly rooted in Christian faith but does not operate as a church or require religious participation; it is intended as a humanitarian sanctuary open to every person regardless of belief.
- **Dignity without conditions.** Faith, prayer, or spiritual participation are never intended to be conditions for dignity, privacy, access, or assistance.
- **Human judgment over automation.** Under the intended model, human stewards — not automated systems — would remain responsible for hardship and relief decisions.
- **Privacy-preserving transparency.** Any future public reporting is designed to publish aggregate information only, never identities, medical information, addresses, or other sensitive individual detail.
- **Permanence of purpose.** Ruaviel is intended to remain under perpetual stewardship, governed by a no-sale, no-capture covenant expressing the commitment that it should not be sold, privatized, or converted into a private commercial enterprise.
- **Say only what's true today.** This document distinguishes what exists and has been tested from what is designed, planned, or intended, and is updated as that boundary actually moves — not in advance of it.
