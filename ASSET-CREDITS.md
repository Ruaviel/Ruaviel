## Licensing Boundary

A purchase or license receipt proves that an asset was legitimately acquired. It does not transfer copyright, and it does not by itself permit redistribution of the original source files. Purchased and licensed assets used in Ruaviel are used under the terms of their respective licenses; the underlying source files remain the property of their original creators and are not published, shared, or redistributed through this repository.

## Assets Under Review

Ruaviel's asset pipeline is undergoing an ongoing provenance and licensing review. Assets whose attribution, licensing terms, or intended use have not been fully confirmed are being evaluated and, where necessary, contained or removed before public deployment. Their inclusion in local development does not represent a claim of ownership or permission beyond their applicable licenses.

## Corrections and Attribution Requests

If you are a creator or rights holder and believe an asset is credited incorrectly, or you have a concern about how an asset is used here, please open an issue on this repository so it can be reviewed and corrected.
